Choose a vehicle. Find your own route.

Find a new route through the city, discover shortcuts or head straight for your next mission. Car Games gives you the freedom to explore a driving world and put a growing collection of vehicles on the road.

On iPhone and iPad, drive online with other players and choose from more than 50 trucks, pickups, buses and classic cars. Complete missions for rewards, buy and upgrade vehicles, or challenge a city boss to a race. PlayStation and Xbox controller support gives you another way to take the wheel.

On Android, explore with cars, buses and trucks, take on driving and racing missions, and buy and upgrade your vehicles. The Android edition includes an offline mode for driving without an internet connection.

Your questions, answered.

Can I use a controller?

The iPhone and iPad edition supports PlayStation and Xbox controllers.

Can I drive offline?

Choose the Android edition for offline driving. The iPhone and iPad edition features online open-world multiplayer driving.

Is Car Games free to download?

The iPhone and iPad app is free to download and offers optional auto-renewing VIP subscriptions. The Android app includes ads and in-app purchases. See your store for current prices and subscription terms.
