Time the jump. Stick the landing.

Hit the ramp, balance your bike in the air and aim for a clean landing. Motorcycle Games: Bike Race brings jumps, obstacles and arcade riding into a session built around timing and control.

On iPhone and iPad, ride desert tracks with ramps, bridges and hazards, then return to timed levels to chase a faster finish. Simple controls make it easy to start, while tricky landings give you something to work on.

On Android, find the game as Motorcycle Race: Bike Game. Ride motorcycle levels with ramps, jumps and obstacles, using simple controls to manage your timing, balance and landings.
