Build your car. Line up for the next race.

Take your car to the starting line and race other players in online multiplayer battles. Racing Clash is built around head-to-head competition: choose your ride, enter a race and chase the next finish.

Build your collection with new cars, upgrade the vehicles you enjoy driving and customize their look. Daily rewards give you another reason to return and prepare for your next race. Play on iPhone, iPad or Android.

Choose Racing Clash when you want a race against another driver. For open-world exploration and driving missions, try Car Games.

Your questions, answered.

Is Racing Clash different from Car Games?

Yes. Racing Clash focuses on head-to-head online racing. Car Games is our open-world driving app with exploration and missions.

Do multiplayer races need an internet connection?

Head-to-head multiplayer races are online. Connect to the internet to race other players.

What can I do with my car collection?

Collect new cars, upgrade your vehicles and customize their look. You can also earn daily rewards as you return to play.

Where can I find support and privacy details?

Read the publisher privacy policy, your store disclosures and contact support if you need help.
