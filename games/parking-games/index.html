Clear the jam, one clever move at a time.

Work out which cars can leave first and open a path through a crowded parking lot. Parking Games turns the parking jam into a colorful 3D puzzle, with increasingly challenging layouts and new skins to unlock as you progress.

Take on more than 10,000 parking puzzles on iPhone and iPad. Play offline on iPhone, iPad and Android, so you can work through your next jam without Wi-Fi.

The Android edition also includes hints and selectable difficulty. Choose Parking Games when you want to plan the order of your moves and enjoy the satisfaction of an empty lot.

Your questions, answered.

What kind of game is Parking Games?

It is a 3D parking-jam and unblock puzzle. Plan which cars can leave first, clear the lot and unlock skins as you progress.

Can I play without Wi-Fi?

Yes. Play the parking puzzles offline on iPhone, iPad and Android. An internet connection is needed for store purchases and other online services.

Are there hints and difficulty choices?

The Android edition includes hints and selectable difficulty.
