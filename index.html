RESEARCH & PLAYER EXPERIENCE

Curious about play.

We’re working with researchers, PhDs and professors affiliated with leading Canadian universities and research institutions, including the Centre for Addiction and Mental Health (CAMH) and Wilfrid Laurier University, to explore mental health, cognitive health and the experience of play.

Our goal is to explore how play might support cognitive health and help people keep their minds engaged as they age. These are research goals, not demonstrated health outcomes.

Research goals do not establish clinical efficacy or institutional endorsement.
